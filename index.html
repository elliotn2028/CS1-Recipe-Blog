Beef Bulgogi Bowl

Description

A rice bowl topped with sweet and savory Korean-style beef and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Thinly sliced beef
  • Cooked rice
  • Soy sauce
  • Brown sugar
  • Garlic
  • Sesame oil
  • Sliced carrots and cucumber

Steps

  1. Cook the rice according to the package instructions.
  2. Mix soy sauce, brown sugar, minced garlic, and sesame oil in a bowl.
  3. Add the thinly sliced beef and let it marinate in the refrigerator for 30 minutes.
  4. Slice the carrots and cucumber.
  5. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the beef in small batches until fully cooked.
  6. Place the rice in a bowl and top with the beef, carrots, and cucumber.

Chicken Pad See Ew

Description

A Thai stir-fried noodle dish made with wide rice noodles, Chinese broccoli, egg, and your choice of protein. The noodles are coated in a savory, slightly sweet soy sauce and seared for a smoky flavor.

Ingredients

  • Wide rice noodles
  • Chicken, beef, or tofu
  • Chinese broccoli
  • Eggs
  • Garlic
  • Light soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Sugar
  • Vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the rice noodles according to the package instructions and drain.
  2. Mix light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar in a small bowl.
  3. Cut your protein and Chinese broccoli into bite-sized pieces. Mince the garlic.
  4. Heat vegetable oil in a wok or large skillet. Add the garlic and protein, and stir-fry until the protein is fully cooked.
  5. Add the Chinese broccoli and stir-fry until tender.
  6. Push everything to one side. Crack in the eggs and scramble until set.
  7. Add the noodles and sauce. Toss gently, letting the noodles sit briefly against the hot pan to brown.
  8. Serve hot.

Garlic Butter Pasta

Description

This pasta combines buttery garlic sauce with Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley. It is a simple meal that comes together quickly.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces of spaghetti
  • 4 tablespoons of butter
  • 4 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup of grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons of chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Add the spaghetti and cook according to the package instructions.
  3. Save one cup of pasta water, then drain the spaghetti.
  4. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium-low heat.
  5. Add the garlic and stir for about one minute, without letting it burn.
  6. Add the spaghetti and toss to coat it in the garlic butter.
  7. Remove from the heat. Add Parmesan and a little pasta water, tossing until a smooth sauce forms.
  8. Top with parsley, season with salt and pepper, and serve.
